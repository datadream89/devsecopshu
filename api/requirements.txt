3. Preserve the two-panel layout

LEFT PANEL — Proactive PreClear

Capability name: Proactive PreClear

Stage label: BEFORE ADJUDICATION

Core promise:

“Identify actionable information gaps earlier.”

Use three concise supporting points:

1. Identify actionable COB information-gap risks using authorized, point-in-time signals.
2. Prioritize cases for human validation and potential correction.
3. Enable earlier intervention before an upcoming adjudication pass, where feasible.

Use an appropriate shield, early-warning, or verification icon.

RIGHT PANEL — Shared Resolution

Capability name: Shared Resolution

Stage label: AFTER A CLAIM PENDS

Core promise:

“Turn fragmented evidence into verified progress.”

Use three concise supporting points:

1. Reconcile relevant records across authorized existing systems.
2. Combine recorded events with processor-validated milestones.
3. Surface confirmed progress, outstanding actions, and next steps.

Use an appropriate connected-records, timeline, or linked-evidence icon.

The existing environment records information across systems such as iView, One View, and Proclaim.

Processors may need to manually navigate those systems and correlate records using claim numbers, member identifiers, image reference numbers, dates of service, and document metadata.

ClaimTrack proposes a unified, verified resolution view—not merely a new claim-status screen.

Do not claim that every source event is already automatically linked.

Do not claim that all events are currently visible to members.

Do not imply that ClaimTrack automatically determines completeness or changes authoritative claim status without validated evidence and approved workflows.

8. Speaker notes

Add the following narration:

“ClaimTrack addresses this challenge through two connected capabilities.

First, Proactive PreClear. Today, COB edits are applied during auto-adjudication. We're proposing an earlier information-readiness assessment using authorized signals, so actionable gaps can be identified and reviewed before an upcoming adjudication pass, where feasible.

Second, Shared Resolution. Relevant information already exists across our systems, but reconstructing the claim's progress can require manual searching and validation.

ClaimTrack proposes bringing those verified events together into one resolution journey—showing what's needed, what's been received, who's responsible, and what happens next.

And importantly, ClaimTrack doesn't replace adjudication. Proclaim remains the authority for claim decisions.

Prevent what we can. Coordinate what remains.”
