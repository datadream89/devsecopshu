ClaimTrack — Redesign Slide 7: Stakeholder Value

Completely redesign Slide 7 ONLY of the existing ClaimTrack presentation.

Objective: Transform Slide 7 from a technical workflow explanation into a compelling, visually elegant, executive-level business value slide suitable for the Cigna Aspire iTournament finals.

This slide must answer one question:

"What becomes better for the people and teams involved in the claims journey?"

1. Strict scope

- Modify ONLY Slide 7.
- Preserve Slides 1–6 and all subsequent slides without modification.
- Preserve the existing slide master, white background, navy-and-green design language, navigation bar with "Value" highlighted, and Cigna Group footer.
- Keep all text, icons, and shapes editable.
- Do not introduce unsupported statistics or claims.

2. Replace the title and subtitle

Title:
"Better experiences. Less operational friction."

Subtitle:
"Four stakeholders. Four potential improvements across the claims journey."

Use a strong, concise navy headline and a smaller, understated subtitle.

3. Remove the current technical workflow

Completely remove:

- Detect → Act → Inform
- The three large workflow panels and connecting arrows
- Technical explanations of risk detection, AI agents, and ticket orchestration
- The large bottom takeaway statement

These concepts are already covered by the solution, prototype, and AI validation slides.

Do not repeat them.

4. Create a premium 2×2 stakeholder value layout

Use four spacious, equally sized areas arranged in a balanced 2×2 grid.

Avoid dense cards or large shaded rectangles. Prefer clean white space, subtle dividers, simple line icons, and restrained accent colors.

Each stakeholder area should contain exactly four elements: one icon, a small stakeholder label, a prominent benefit headline, and one short supporting statement.

Less uncertainty

Stakeholder: PROVIDERS

Benefit headline:

BOTTOM LEFT — CLAIMS OPERATIONS

BOTTOM RIGHT — THE CIGNA GROUP

Icon: Business performance or efficiency

Stakeholder: THE CIGNA GROUP

Benefit headline:
Measurable efficiency

Supporting statement:
"Test reductions in contacts and rework."

Use navy for the primary benefit headlines and a restrained green accent for The Cigna Group.

Make the benefit headlines visually dominant, not the stakeholder labels.

5. Add one discreet validation note

Near the bottom of the slide, above the existing footer, include:

"Potential benefits to be evaluated through a controlled pilot."

Use small but presentation-readable gray text.

Do not add any other disclaimers, footnotes, or takeaway banners.

6. Critical content boundaries

- Do not mention the $6.7M cost pool, $335K, $1M, or financial scenarios. These belong on Slide 8.
- Do not repeat Proactive PreClear and Shared Resolution technical descriptions.
- Do not include AI model details, rules, GenAI, agent architecture, or ticket-creation diagrams.
- Do not imply that operational improvements have already been demonstrated.
- Do not promise faster claim payments, fewer denials, or guaranteed savings.
- Do not imply that automated tickets replace human processor judgment.
- Do not add additional stakeholder categories or paragraphs.

7. Design direction

The slide should feel like a premium Fortune 100 executive strategy presentation, not a technical architecture diagram.

Prioritize:

- Generous whitespace
- Four large, instantly understandable benefit statements
- Consistent iconography
- Strong typographic hierarchy
- Precise alignment and equal spacing
- Minimal visual clutter
- Readability from the back of a conference room
- Restrained navy, white, and green palette

Avoid:

- Dense bullet lists
- Heavy borders or shadows
- Excessive text
- Decorative illustrations
- Complex diagrams
- Repetitive callout boxes
- Small, difficult-to-read descriptions

8. Final quality requirements

Before finalizing, verify that:

1. The slide is understandable within five seconds.
2. The four stakeholder benefits are visually distinct.
3. The slide does not repeat the technical content of Slides 4–6.
4. The language describes potential value, not proven outcomes.
5. No financial figures duplicate Slide 8.
6. The slide remains visually consistent with the rest of the presentation.
7. All four benefit headlines are large and immediately readable.

Final instruction: Deliver a substantial redesign of Slide 7, emphasizing business impact and stakeholder outcomes rather than technology. Make it visually memorable, concise, credible, and presentation-ready.
