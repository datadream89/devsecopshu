ClaimTrack — Slide 7: Value Creation

Task: Completely redesign Slide 7 ONLY.

The current slide is titled "Better experiences. Less operational friction." and uses four sparse stakeholder quadrants.

Replace that design with a compelling, executive-level Value Creation slide for the Cigna Aspire iTournament finals.

The slide must demonstrate not just what ClaimTrack does, but why its potential outcomes matter to members, providers, claims operations, and the business.

1. Headline

Title:
"Less chasing. Less rework. More capacity for care."

Subtitle:
"Three value drivers. Measurable outcomes. Human oversight."

Use a prominent navy headline and a restrained gray subtitle.

The title represents intended benefits, not achieved results.

2. Main visual — Three business value drivers

Replace the four-quadrant layout with three substantial horizontal sections, stacked vertically.

Each section should have:

- A simple, distinctive line icon.
- A small uppercase value category.
- One large, memorable benefit statement.
- One concise explanation of the business significance.

Use strong typographic hierarchy and balanced spacing.

01 — EXPERIENCE VALUE

Reduce the need to chase answers

Verified claim status, ownership, and next steps may reduce member and provider follow-up.

Business significance:
Less uncertainty and fewer repetitive status inquiries.

Icon: Member/provider communication or verified status.

02 — OPERATIONAL VALUE

Prevent avoidable downstream work

Earlier, prioritized processor review may reduce repeat pends, duplicate touches, and reprocessing.

Business significance:
More focused processor effort and potentially less rework.

Icon: Claims checklist or workflow improvement.

03 — ECONOMIC VALUE

Release capacity for higher-value work

Measure reductions in contact demand and rework against the additional effort required for proactive reviews.

Business significance:
Net operational value, not just gross activity reduction.

Icon: Efficiency, capacity, or performance.

3. Add a concise measurement strip

Below the three value drivers, include one clean, horizontally organized measurement strip.

Heading:

HOW WE WILL MEASURE VALUE

Display four short indicators with small icons or separators:

Repeat contacts | Repeat pends | Processor effort | Verified interventions

These are proposed pilot measures, not demonstrated improvements.

The measurement strip should be visually distinct but understated.

4. Design direction

Create a polished Fortune 100 executive presentation slide.

Use:

- White background.
- Deep navy headings.
- Restrained green accents.
- Three substantial horizontal value sections.
- Consistent, minimalist line icons.
- Generous but purposeful whitespace.
- Large, presentation-readable typography.
- Subtle separators rather than heavy boxes.
- A clean measurement strip at the bottom.

Make the slide visually engaging without overcrowding it.

The three value sections should occupy most of the available content area.

Avoid creating another sparse slide with excessive empty space.

5. Content accuracy

All value claims must be framed as potential outcomes to be tested.

Do not claim that ClaimTrack has already:

- Reduced contacts.
- Prevented pends.
- Reduced rework.
- Released operational capacity.
- Generated savings.

The economic value must account for the incremental cost of human review and any additional operational workload.

Do not suggest that AI independently adjudicates claims or authorizes corrective action.

Add one small, readable qualifier:

"Expected benefits subject to controlled pilot validation."

6. Avoid duplication

Slide 4 explains the two solution capabilities.

Slide 5 demonstrates the prototype, including proposed agent-created review tickets.

Slide 6 explains AI validation.

Slide 8 presents the financial opportunity and illustrative savings scenarios.

Therefore, Slide 7 must focus on value drivers and measurable business outcomes, not technology or financial calculations.

Do not include:

- Detect → Act → Inform workflows.
- AI model architecture.
- Detailed agent or ticket-routing diagrams.
- Technical descriptions of Proactive PreClear or Shared Resolution.
- The $6.7M cost pool.
- The $335K or $1M financial scenarios.
- The $250K pilot investment.
- Dense bullet lists or lengthy paragraphs.

Before finalizing, confirm that:

1. The slide communicates three distinct sources of business value.
2. Each value driver has a clear operational or customer significance.
3. The slide explains why ClaimTrack could matter beyond its technical capabilities.
4. The four pilot measures are immediately visible.
