PROJECT OVERVIEW

ClaimTrack has two capabilities:

1. Proactive PreClear
Identifies claims at risk of information-related processing interruptions BEFORE adjudication.

It covers:
A. FIRST-PASS PEND RISK: Predict whether a new claim may pend during its initial adjudication.
B. REPEAT-PEND RISK: Predict whether a previously pended claim may pend again when it re-enters adjudication.

The purpose is to identify potentially actionable issues early and route them for targeted human review. A high risk score does not prove a pend is preventable.

2. Shared Resolution
Coordinates actions and verified milestones after a claim has already pended, particularly when member COB information is required.

The provider can see the claim's pending reason, the responsible party, and verified progress milestones. Member submission and authorized verification are separate actions. Verification does not automatically result in claim payment.

PROCLAIM BUSINESS DEFINITIONS

Disposition S = Final Disposition: Disallowed Pending Information.
Disposition K = Final Disposition: Paid Claim.

For this POC, define:

FIRST-PASS PEND:
A claim encounters an information-related interruption during its first adjudication cycle.

SECOND PASS:
A claim that previously pended or received an adjudication decision undergoes processor action and re-enters adjudication.

REPEAT PEND:
A claim pended during its first pass, underwent processor action, re-entered adjudication, and pended again during its second pass.

In our synthetic repeat-pend example, the second-pass outcome is S, indicating another information-related disallowance requiring additional information.

Assume the described ProClaim second-pass pend dataset represents this workflow, but document that the source population and line-versus-claim granularity require later business validation.

Reason descriptions can include:
- COB applied incorrectly
- Provider billing error
- System adjustment applied
- 90-day rule
- Additional information received

These are examples of processing reasons, not guaranteed mappings to disposition or proof of avoidability. Use fictional reason-code identifiers.

IMPORTANT POINT-IN-TIME RULE

PreClear makes its assessment BEFORE the adjudication outcome of the cycle being predicted.

For first-pass prediction, do not use first-pass disposition, payment information, or reason codes generated by first-pass adjudication.

For repeat-pend prediction, historical first-pass information may be used if it was available before the second-pass assessment. Never use the second-pass outcome or reason codes generated afterward.

Maintain separate assessment-time features and subsequent outcome events.
